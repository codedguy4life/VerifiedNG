GETTING STARTED

How VerifiedNG Works

It's easy to get started. Here's how it works.

1

Create your free account

Sign up with your details. You can add your area to find things near you, but you don't have to.

2

Find what you need

Look through posts, services, things for sale, local news and groups. Read the details before you decide.

3

Talk and connect
